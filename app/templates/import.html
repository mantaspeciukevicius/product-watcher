Send listings from your browser

For Chrome, Edge or Brave on your PC. The extension reads only the marketplace tab you open and sends your reviewed selection to this tracker.

  1. Download your paired extension, then extract the ZIP.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the extracted product-watcher-extension folder.
  3. Pin Product Watcher — Send to Tracker. Open its popup and press Connect to allow access to this local tracker. The download already contains your server address and import token.
  4. Open a Vinted, Skelbiu or Allegro search or listing page normally. Click the extension, inspect the page, review/edit the listings, then send your selection.

The paired ZIP contains your private import token: keep it private. No marketplace cookies, passwords or account sessions are sent. Imports appear within roughly five seconds and do not trigger marketplace searches. PLN imports wait for a valid exchange rate. Disabled sources are skipped. Normal Android Chrome does not support this desktop extension; the import form remains available on your phone.
